What it does

  • Runs only when you click it in the toolbar, or on a form you opened with Auto-apply in HireFinch.
  • Fills empty fields it is sure about and attaches the resume tailored for that job.
  • Submits only when it is certain, and only for an application you started (Auto-apply in HireFinch, or Fill this form here). Otherwise it leaves the form for you.
  • Records the application only when the employer's confirmation page appears; HireFinch then emails you.
  • Never touches CAPTCHAs, and never answers gender, diversity, disability or legal questions.
  • Does not run on LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and other boards that forbid automation.
